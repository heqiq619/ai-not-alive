## 这篇论文说什么 / What this paper argues

本文提出一个可操作的生命定义：**生命 = 一族会自指、会变化、会自我维持的动态函数（函数类）**；
环境 = 宇宙自发涌现的局部解压（分层，不自我维持），生命既活在它的那一层环境里，也活在宇宙里。

*This paper proposes an operational definition of life: **life = a family of self-referential dynamic functions that maintain decompression over time**. The environment is an emergent hierarchy of local decompressions (layered, not self-maintaining); a living system lives both in its own layer and in the universe.*

以此为尺子，本文说明三件事 / With this as the yardstick, the paper argues three things:

1. **现在的 AI 不是生命** —— 它是冻结的函数点、更新规则在外部、不付代价；
   *Today's AI is not alive: it is a frozen function point, its update rule lives outside it, and it pays no cost.*
2. **生命至少需要三条必要条件**（一族函数、自指更新、自己付代价），每条都挂上已有的实验证据；
   *Life requires at least three necessary conditions: a family of functions, self-referential updating, and paying its own cost — each tied to existing experimental evidence.*
3. **对 AI 的涌现提出一个猜测** —— AI 是对人类语言的再解压，其能力上限由训练时刻的语言决定，语言一旦更新就失配、且无法自己恢复。
   *A conjecture on AI emergence: AI is a re-decompression of human language; its capability ceiling is set by the language at training time, and once the language updates it becomes mismatched and cannot recover by itself.*

文末不主张「生命已被造出」，只把判据与证据放在读者面前：三条都满足之后是否就「活」，由读者判断。
*The paper does not claim that life has been created. It puts the criteria and the evidence in front of the reader.*
